# Arena hero artwork

The Arena overview hero shows a crossed cricket bat and padel racket beside its headline
on desktop (1024px and wider), hidden on smaller screens. The equipment artwork has a
transparent background, with no surrounding glow, ring or shadow.

Asset: `public/images/arena/bat-and-padel.webp`. Created with the built-in imagegen tool,
then optimized to WebP. The artwork is decorative and hidden from screen readers.

Prompt: a realistic light-willow cricket bat and perforated carbon padel racket crossed
in a balanced X on transparent alpha, complete objects in frame. Padel face upper-right,
bat blade upper-left, handles toward the bottom. Premium product photography with lime
rim light (#d5ff3f), ivory highlights, charcoal carbon textures and natural wood. No
strings, text, brand names, logos, floor, people or additional equipment.
